This is the torque ceiling, not a print acceleration. It answers one question — at what acceleration does a motor hit its configured torque limit — and ignores everything that binds first on a real machine: belt stretch and gantry resonance, the planner’s own max_accel and corner budget, and whether the regenerative resistor can absorb the deceleration. Treat a number here as the wall you cannot pass, not the speed you will print at.
