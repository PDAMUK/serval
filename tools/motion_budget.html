This is the torque ceiling, not a print acceleration. The peak is what the drive will deliver for the milliseconds a move accelerates; the continuous figure is what the belt or the motor will hold for hours. Neither is a failure point: above the belt’s continuous rating the wall is tooth ratcheting, which depends on installation tension and teeth in mesh, and nothing here puts a number on it. The page also ignores everything that binds first on a real machine: belt stretch (the belt controls set what a belt can carry, not how stiff it is) and gantry resonance, the planner’s own max_accel and corner budget, and whether the regenerative resistor can absorb the deceleration. Treat a number here as the wall you cannot pass, not the speed you will print at.
